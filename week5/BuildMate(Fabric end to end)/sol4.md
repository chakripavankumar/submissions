# Revenue and Fleet Status — June 2026

Hadapsar Depot generated the highest total revenue at approximately ₹1.08 crore, making it the leading depot by overall revenue during June. However, Talegaon Depot generated the highest revenue per billed rental at ₹90,920, indicating the strongest revenue per rental transaction. Across all six depots, 175 rentals were still out, with Chakan Depot having the highest number of currently-out rentals at 45, followed by Hadapsar Depot with 40. These insights were possible by joining billing records to rental records and then to the depot dimension, because no single source contained all the required information. In the Silver layer, we preserved rentals with missing check-in timestamps by treating a null quality flag as not proven bad; a careless null-unsafe filter would have removed these valid still-out rentals and made the currently-out count zero.
